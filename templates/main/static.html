ABOUT US

Local discovery with a service marketplace built in.

LocalConnect helps customers discover local professionals and businesses, compare profiles and reviews, and send service requests from one place.

Discover

Search by service, category and selected service area.

Compare

Review services, prices, ratings and LocalConnect verification status.

Request

Send and track service requests with a complete status history.

Verification on LocalConnect means the profile was approved by a platform administrator. It does not represent government certification or a professional licence.

Privacy Policy

Development MVP policy template — update with legal counsel before production launch.

Information we store

LocalConnect stores account information, selected service-area/location fields, profile information, service requests, reviews, favorites, notifications and moderation records needed to operate the platform.

Location

Precise latitude and longitude are optional. The MVP primarily uses manually entered city, state, area and pincode information and does not calculate fake distances.

Security

Passwords are stored as password hashes. Application forms use validation, output escaping, prepared database statements and CSRF protection where applicable.

Before production

Configure a production privacy policy, retention schedule, cookie disclosures, support contact and applicable Indian data-protection compliance before accepting real users.

Terms of Use

Development MVP terms template — replace with reviewed production terms before launch.

Marketplace role

LocalConnect helps users discover and contact independent local providers and businesses. A LocalConnect verification badge only reflects platform-admin approval and is not a government licence or professional certification.

User responsibilities

Users must provide accurate information, use service requests lawfully, avoid spam or abusive content, and respect other users' privacy.

Reviews and moderation

Reviews are tied to completed service requests. Reported profiles, businesses and reviews may be investigated or moderated by administrators.

Payments

The current MVP contains payment-ready records but does not process real payments until a payment gateway is configured.
